Don't Touch Red - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Don't Touch Red ("the game") is a mobile game by Ankit Paudel. This policy explains what information the game and its ads use.

What stays on your device

There are no accounts and no game server. Your scores, skins, settings and progress are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch to revive. Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads. This may include personalised ads, depending on your choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where required (for example in the EU, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before ads are requested. You can reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
